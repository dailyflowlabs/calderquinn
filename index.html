The Artist

Working-Class Poetry. Unvarnished Soul.

Born and raised near the timber hills and quiet creeks of the Appalachian foothills, Calder Quinn writes songs for people who know the weight of a long shift and the silence of an empty kitchen. With a beat-up acoustic guitar, a broken-in baseball cap pulled low, and a heavy five o'clock shadow, Calder delivers heartland Americana stripped down to its honest bone.

He doesn't sing about polished city lights. His lyrics live in coffee rings on hardware store counters, dusty pickup tailgates at twilight, and late-night drives where you talk to headlights on the wall. When his voice climbs from a low, gravelly whisper into a full chest-voice roar on "Leave the Light in the Hollow", you feel every mile of road behind him.
