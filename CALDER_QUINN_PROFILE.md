### 🌟 ARTIST OVERVIEW
* **Artist Name:** Calder Quinn
* **Genre:** Gritty Heartland Americana / Rustbelt Country / Soulful Appalachian Folk-Rock
* **Age:** 30
* **Hometown:** Pine Hollow, Appalachian foothills
* **Vibe:** Rugged, quietly handsome, blue-collar poet with a gravel-and-honey singing belt. Wears a broken-in baseball cap pulled low, heavy 5 o'clock shadow, and hands that look like they've spent years turning wrenches or working mill shifts before picking up a worn acoustic guitar. Authentic, honest, and unpolished.
* **Sound Reference:** Zach Bryan raw chest-voice emotion meets Tyler Childers Appalachian storytelling, Noah Kahan soul, and Chris Stapleton melodic power.

### 🎵 CORE DISCOGRAPHY & LYRIC BLUEPRINTS

#### 1. "Leave the Light in the Hollow" (Lead Video Single)
* **Duration:** 3:32
* **Tempo:** 78 BPM, building to an explosive anthemic belted chorus
* **Vibe:** Massive emotional crescendo — low gravelly verse into a roof-shaking raw vocal belt.
* **Lyrics Snippet:**
  ```text
  [Verse 1]
  I keep my voice down in the morning
  Like the house might break if I speak
  Coffee black as the county line
  And a quiet I don't try to keep...

  [Pre-Chorus]
  I can hold it in the kitchen
  I can hold it in the truck
  But the second that night hits the ridgeline...

  [Chorus]
  I leave the light in the hollow
  So I know where the dark begins
  I throw my whole damn chest at heaven
  Just to hear it throw it back again
  If I go quiet I disappear
  So I sing it till it thins
  Leave the light in the hollow
  And I crawl back in.
  ```

* **Duration:** 3:17
* **Tempo:** 72 BPM acoustic folk-ballad

#### 3. "Halfway Home"
* **Duration:** 1:28
* **Tempo:** 116 BPM driving heartland rhythm
